# Privacy Statement

FortSight AI uses your device's location and compass orientation to provide a tactical map view of nearby historical forts.

## Data Usage
- **GPS Location:** Used exclusively on your device to calculate the distance and bearing to nearby forts.
- **Compass Heading:** Used exclusively on your device to orient the map and determine which forts you are currently facing.

## Data Storage & Sharing
- Your location and orientation data are **never** sent to our servers.
- The data is processed locally within your browser.
- We do not track your movements or store your location history.

## Permissions
The app will request permissions for:
1. **Location Services:** To determine your coordinates.
2. **Motion and Orientation:** To determine your device's compass heading (especially on iOS devices).

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your browser or device settings. If you choose not to grant these permissions, you can still use the app with a manually inputted location (via the Dev Panel) or explore the default view.
